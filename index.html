Myeonghun Jeong

Staff Engineer · Samsung Electronics AI Center  |  Seoul, Republic of Korea

I earned my Ph.D. from Seoul National University under the supervision of Prof. Nam Soo Kim, with a primary focus on speech synthesis and deep generative models. Since March 2025, I have been working at Samsung Electronics, where I research AI for material discovery — post-training large language models (LLMs) as core reasoning engines using techniques such as supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and in-context learning (ICL).
